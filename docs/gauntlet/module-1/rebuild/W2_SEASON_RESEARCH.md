## 3. THE APRON RESTRICTION — §7.2 RESOLVED

**FIRST apron. Not the second.** `cbaguide.com/thresholds/apron`, Apron Limitations Table row D
(read 2026-09-04), verbatim: *"Signs (i) a waived Player (ii) during the Regular Season (iii)
whose waived Salary exceeded the NTMLE amount — **First Apron**."* Independently corroborated
by hoopsrumors' *2026 NBA Buyout Market Watch* (2026-02-11): *"a player whose pre-waiver salary
exceeds the non-taxpayer mid-level exception is ineligible to sign with a team operating over
**either** tax apron."* The 2025 edition names Ben Simmons as the worked case. §7.2's R3 reading
(second apron) is a transcription error; the same page also settles the pick penalty as
**7 drafts out, 2-of-4**, not 3-of-5.

Three things the spec must get right, because the same page states them:

1. **The test is the position AFTER the transaction**, not before ("if the Team's Apron Team
   Salary exceeds the applicable Apron Threshold **after executing** the transaction").
2. **The threshold is Apron Team Salary**, which is Team Salary **minus cap holds** plus unlikely
   bonuses and several adjustments. `world.ts` carries `committed` **including** holds — Detroit's
   holds are $28,834,548. Testing the apron against `committed` would refuse Detroit signings the
   real rule allows.
3. The comparison figure is the **current** league year's NTMLE: **$15,044,000** for 2026-27
   (2025-26 was $14,104,000; 2024-25 was $12,822,000). Every February card above must be scored
   against $15,044,000 when it is staged into 2026-27.

**5-6 simplification.** Print one line: *"Past the first apron you may not sign a player who was
cut during the season if he was earning more than $15,044,000."* No exception name, no acronym.
**Misconception risks:** (a) that the apron blocks *all* February signing — it blocks exactly one
class of player, which is why no desk is ever stuck; mitigate by having the refusal message name
the player's own pre-waiver salary and show what remains legal. (b) that the check runs on the
payroll the student sees — mitigate by computing post-transaction and by never showing two payroll
definitions on one screen (BC-7).

## 4. TEN-DAY CONTRACTS

`cbaguide.com/contractanatomy/contracttypes` (read 2026-09-04). **Open January 5 of each season
→ January 5, 2027** for 2026-27 (a rule, not a schedule date). Length: the longer of ten days or
three games. Salary: the **minimum, prorated per day**. Limit: no team may sign one player to
more than **two**. Roster limit, by players on the Active/Inactive List including the ten-days:
**12 → 0 permitted · 13 → 1 · 14 → 2 · 15 → 3.**

**The cost is not a minimum contract, and this matters to Shocks A and C.** cbaguide's worked
example: Elfrid Payton's 10-day paid $171,756 and charged **$119,972** to cap, apron and tax,
because a minimum player's charge is subsidised to the two-years-of-service minimum. Real
February rest-of-season minimums in 2025-26 charged **$514,682** (Tyus Jones, Denver, 2026-03-05)
and **$593,864** (Mason Plumlee, San Antonio, 2026-02-27). **A January ten-day is roughly a
twentieth of a full minimum, and a late-February signing roughly a quarter of one.** If Week 2
charges a full-season minimum for either, it overstates the tax clock several-fold and understates
the option-value lesson it exists to teach.

## 5. THE TAX CLOCK

Confirmed and tightened. `cbaguide.com/thresholds/luxurytax` (read 2026-09-04): *"Tax Salary is
determined **as of the start of the Team's last Regular Season game**, subject to a couple of
minor adjustments made after the Regular Season."* Penalties apply *"if at the end of the Regular
Season the Team's Tax Salary exceeds the Tax Threshold."* 2026-27 tax line **$200,428,000**;
bracket width $6,064,000; entry rate $1.00, repeater $3.00 — all matching `world.ts` and §2.2.
This replaces §2.2's single medium-confidence source with a rules source. **The exact date of the
last regular-season game is NOT VERIFIED** (nba.com 403).

**Free and real: March 1 is the date that makes February a window.** A player waived after
11:59 p.m. on March 1 is **postseason-ineligible with a new team**
(`cbaguide.com/eligibility/rosters`, read 2026-09-04; hoopsrumors 2026-02-27 states it as the
operative deadline in live reporting). That is why every card in §2 was cut in February. Week 2's
ADAPT round should print it.
