## The shared visual system across all three

- **Ground:** near-black navy, roughly `#07060d`–`#0b0a14`. Never pure black.
- **Surfaces:** panels lift 3–6% above ground with a 1px hairline border at
  ~8–12% white. No drop shadows doing the lifting; the border does it.
- **Primary accent:** violet `#7c5cff`-ish, used for the active nav item, the
  primary CTA gradient, progress fill, and the eyebrow label.
- **Secondary accent:** gold/amber, reserved for *committed* and *climactic* —
  the TOTAL field, starter borders, the you-row, the Enter Board Room button.
- **Semantic:** green good / amber caution / red urgent-or-danger. Used sparingly
  and always with a word, never colour alone.
- **Type:** heavy condensed caps for franchise/display names; small letterspaced
  all-caps for panel labels; tabular lining numerals for every dollar figure.
- **Density:** high, but organised into hairline-separated cells. This is
  television information design, not a spreadsheet.
- **Photography:** every screen has exactly one or two photographic beats (war
  room, arena crowd, arena exterior) heavily darkened and used as environment,
  never as content.

## WHAT WE TAKE

Ranked by value.

1. **The persistent left rail as the front office itself.** Same rail every
   lesson; sections light as the module reaches them. This is the single best
   idea in the three mockups and it is exactly the founder's §11 "spatial /
   persistent product memory" — achievable with zero 3D.
2. **The offer composer docked *inside* the player card.** The decision happens
   on the player, not in a detached form. The player is the object of desire;
   the money is an attribute of wanting him.
3. **`Max Offer: 3 yrs / $91.3M` printed under the button.** The constraint,
   stated as a number, at the point of decision. Our `TOOLS` already compute
   this truthfully — we can print it honestly today.
4. **RECENT ACTIVITY / PLAYERS OFF THE BOARD as the class's own moves.** The
   news feed is the room. Truthful, computed, and it is what makes sixteen desks
   feel like one league.
5. **The two-sided trade card** (`X RECEIVES` | `Y RECEIVES`). The clearest
   rendering of a trade that exists. Adopt wholesale.
6. **CAP OVERVIEW with all four thresholds plus this team's space against each.**
   We verified all four. Printing them is safe and it is the whole lesson made
   ambient.
7. **The four-node round rail** (Planning / Offseason / Season / Defense). The
   three-act arc made visible on every screen, in every lesson.
8. **MULTIPLE WAYS TO WIN as five tiles.** Exactly the no-single-champion
   mandate, rendered. Keep the tiles.
9. **The board's four defense tabs** — Strategic Rationale / Alternative Paths /
   Risk & Reward / What I'd Do Differently. This is nearly a verbatim spec for
   the final defense and it is good.
10. **TEAM NEEDS ranked with an impact word.** Our engine already has `openJobs`;
    this is an honest render of state we hold.
11. **DRAFT ASSETS as a plain checkable list.** Real, verifiable, and the
    currency of Act 3.
12. **The attention triad — Phone / Inbox / Scouting with counts.** Pending
    attention as a first-class object beats a "Next" button.
13. **One cinematic hero beat per screen**, with a single display line over it.
14. **Tabular numerals + hairline cells** for all money.

## WHAT WE REFUSE

Each of these is in the mockups. Each is forbidden unless the stated condition
is met. Founder said the product "must earn every element" and "ECONOMIC TRUTH >
MOCKUP".

| Element in mockup | Why refused | Condition to admit it |
|---|---|---|
| `TEAM OVERALL 89`, `Star Power 91`, `Depth Score 78`, `OVR 96` per player, `FAN & MARKET HEALTH 92`, `MARKET TEMPERATURE HOT`, `Evidence Strength` | Manufactured metrics. A single-number team rating is the exact false lesson the old module computed (see `ECONOMIC_CONTRACT.md` §0) — it makes "best team" a scalar and reinstates the intuition the whole module exists to break. | Never for a composite team rating. A component figure is admissible only if honestly computed from state and printed with its definition. |
| `Level 1 / 150 XP / Rookie Manager` | Violates D4 outright. No XP, levels, badges, progression. | Never. |
| `CLASS MARKET PULSE — WHO'S WINNING RIGHT NOW?`, ranked 1–6 | Violates the no-universal-champion rule and the founder's own §16 in the same brief. A live class ranking also tells a kid they are losing while they still have moves. | Never as a ranking. Replaced by non-ordered strategy contrast (see below). |
| `Wins Above Expectation +6.2`, `Clutch Performance 62%`, `Opposition Strength Top 6` | Invented statistics presented as evidence. Also violates the 5–6 no-percentages profile. | Only if the simulation genuinely produces them and they are defined on screen. |
| Player headshots, team logos, jersey art, the arena photograph, `CRYPTO.COM ARENA` | Founder §4: do not rip proprietary visual assets, headshots, or broadcast packages for authenticity. Full offer detail at the reveal. |

## THE ONE SUBSTITUTION THAT MATTERS

Mockup B's ranked `WHO'S WINNING RIGHT NOW` is the most attractive and most
dangerous element in the set. It is what makes the projector feel alive, and it
is exactly what the founder forbids two sections later.

Replacement, honestly computable from state we already hold: **THE ROOM DISAGREES.**
Same slot, same energy, no ranking — the projector shows the widest genuine
*disagreement* in the class right now. "Six desks are chasing the same centre."
"Half the room has spent its flexibility; half has not spent a dollar."
"Two desks want the same player for opposite reasons." Every line is a computed
fact about the class, no desk is above another, and every line is an argument
the teacher can open immediately. It makes kids look up harder than a
leaderboard does, because the question is unresolved.
