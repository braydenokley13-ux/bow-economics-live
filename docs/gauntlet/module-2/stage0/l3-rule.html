WRITING THE RULE — the rule-writing loop, then living under it (Design C, L3 Stage-0)

Six desks. Two dials: SHARE (what fraction of local revenue pools and splits equally) and CONDITION (must you reinvest at least 15% to collect your full pool cut?). Three 90-second offer rounds, anonymous histogram between rounds, two-thirds-near-median adoption. Then you live one week under the old rule and one under the new one.
