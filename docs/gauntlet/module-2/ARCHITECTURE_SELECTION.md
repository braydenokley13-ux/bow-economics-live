# Module 2 Architecture Selection — Wave 1 decision record

Boss run `m2-quality-war`, Level 3 Quality War. Decision by lead-integrator on the
evidence below. No selection meeting was held: the three independent selection
reviews did not conflict on the winner, so a meeting would have been ceremony
(all three rankings, produced without sight of each other, put the same
candidate first or its L1 first).

## The war

Three genuinely competing architectures, produced from shared inputs
(SPORTS_REALITY_INPUT.md, ECONOMIC_CONTRACT.md):

- **A — DESIGN_A_BOXOFFICE_EVOLVED.md** (PRICE / BOOK / LEGISLATE): the
  strongest module an evolved Price Dial could anchor, with a Replay Dial
  repair for the recorded fake-uncertainty dissent.
- **B — DESIGN_B_REFOUND.md** (SELL / HOST / RULE): structural refound; the
  student never sets a price — inventory release, hosting, rule-writing.
- **C — DESIGN_C_FIRSTPRINCIPLES.md** (price the night / host the league /
  write the rule): clean-room design produced with the existing prototype
  deliberately withheld from its context (redacted inputs; protocol held).

Five Stage-0 prototypes were built and independently played (all three rival
L1 loops; the convergent L2 hosting loop; the convergent L3 rule loop), plus a
deterministic brute-force harness for the sharing arithmetic every candidate's
L3 leaned on.

| Economic Truth (`sel-econ-review`) | **C > B > A**. A blocked outright at mechanism level (dissent `econ-candidate-a-blocked`): its L1 shifters leave the argmax invariant ("lock the Replay peak" dominant) and its L3 take function has no cross-seat term (teaches sharing-as-pure-cost). |
| Gameplay (`play-review`, `l3-replay-review`) | C's L1 blind-price loop **STRONG** — the only STRONG in the war (nothing sweepable pre-commit, no dominant line under five attack strategies, best felt beat: the Night-5 repeat card; best reveal: Two Peaks). A's L1 **WEAK** with a false-cause closing reveal; B's L1 **WEAK**. L2 hosting **FUNCTIONAL**. L3 rule loop **FUNCTIONAL** after a UI repair, selectable with named fixes. |
| Sharing arithmetic (`l3-arith-harness`, exit 1) | Refuted the naive shared L3 signature: sharing never moves a capacity-saturated market's optimal price, and a uniformly-taxed all-revenue base moves no argmax. Dissent `l3-sharing-argmax` upheld with a closed-form discharge path. |

## Decision

**Candidate C — DESIGN_C_FIRSTPRINCIPLES.md — is Module 2's architecture.**
Three lessons: **L1 "Full House"** (price the night, blind commit, history-only
feedback, CASH/RENEWALS two-book scoreboard), **L2 "You Don't Play Alone"**
(host the league; visitor draw fills your building; reinvest-in-Draw dial),
**L3 "Writing the Rule"** (rule dials → offer rounds → two-thirds adoption →
live under the adopted rule; operate-a-given-real-rule fallback), with the
Kings 22-8 Board-of-Governors capstone.

Why C: first on real-world strength (the only design that has students operate
real situations), first on economic truth (the only falsifiable design — it
published its constants — and the only L3 signature that survives the
arithmetic refutation via its differential reinvest instrument), and owner of
the war's only STRONG loop at the exact slot (L1) where a module lives or dies.

## Killed

- **Candidate A's module**, including the Replay Dial: play evidence showed a
  dominant line and a closing reveal that blames the student for a shock the
  model applied silently; econ review showed both its L1 and L3 payloads false
  in its own model. Killed, not repaired — its load-bearing bet failed on the
  bench, which is what the bench is for.
- **Candidate B's module**: truest L2 instrument in the war (visitor's-share
  dial) but WEAK L1 play, an L1 premise econ review found false (seats as a
  season-long stock), unfalsifiable constants, and last place on real-world
  integration.
- **The existing Box Office lesson** (`m2-box-office` module + PROTOTYPE_SPEC):
  superseded as a lesson. Dissent `econ-boxoffice-unrepaired` is resolved by
  this kill — the model it indicts does not carry forward. Code removal is
  deferred to the build wave (the runtime module is dormant and harmless until
  then).

## Survives from the Box Office (as patterns, not code)

Operate-don't-choose discipline; path-dependence delivered as a changed
*starting state*; solo-operator-plus-class-aggregate structure;
fictional-franchise crest device for board privacy; server-authoritative pure
demand functions with constants never serialized to views; teacher-fallback
reveal discipline. C's design already embodies all of these.

## Binding build charter (the build wave contract inherits these)

Falsifiable conditions the built module must satisfy, aggregated from the
three reviews — the build is not done while any is unmet:

1. **BC-1 (econ, discharges `l3-sharing-argmax`):** L3's signature instrument
   is the *differential* reinvest response (r* ∝ (1−s)², verified moving
   ≥2 dial steps across adopted shares), never a uniform price drop; at least
   one capacity-saturated big market whose *non-movement* is itself taught
   (moving arrow beside flat arrow, "why didn't New York move?"); any taxed
   revenue base coexists with an untaxed stream so the arithmetic can move.
2. **BC-2 (econ):** C's constant defects repaired: R6 asymmetry (16.5× against
   the low price at New York) and R8 (Memphis capped at 75.3% fill) — retuned
   and re-verified by re-running the harness family green.
3. **BC-3 (SR):** C-1 fixed (no worst-picks-first claim; lottery reality);
   C-2 pipe magnitudes re-derived consistently with the gate-share ledger and
   SR-1; season stamps on every real figure.
4. **BC-4 (play):** L1 preserves the verified STRONG loop as built in the
   prototype: nothing sweepable pre-commit, the Night-1-card repeat beat, and
   the Two Peaks reveal.
5. **BC-5 (play):** L2 must exceed FUNCTIONAL at the built-lesson gate; the
   decomposition reveal must let a player attribute a night's gate correctly
   from the UI alone.
6. **BC-6 (play):** L3 fixes: the CONDITION control must be a real, adopted,
   consequence-bearing decision; the voter's club identity visible while
   voting; a paid-in/received transfer column so gains are attributable to
   transfer vs own dial; proposal histogram withheld until after round 1
   (anti-herding, per C's own design); the real-rule fallback retained.
7. **BC-7 (module):** the three-surface classroom build (play/teach/board),
   teacher fallbacks, board privacy, and the D4/D6 invariants — gated by the
   full required-role roster at the wave gate, which Wave 1 deliberately did
   not exercise.

Open human-threshold questions (spoken reasoning, adoption timing,
economic-vs-moral talk) are *not* claimed by this selection; they belong to
the classroom rung of RAMAZ_READINESS.md.
