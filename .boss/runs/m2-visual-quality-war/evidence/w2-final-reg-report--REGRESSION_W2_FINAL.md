## regressions-found

**1. IMPORTANT — the settled-night / carried-dial copy attributes a teacher auto-commit to the pair.**
Desk 3 (`Ari & Tal`) chose $70 on Night 1 and then never pressed NEXT again. The teacher's bell
auto-committed its Nights 2 and 3 at the plan price $24. When the desk finally reached the Night 4
dial view, the screen read `Your dial is at $24 — the price you charged on Night 3.` (OBSERVED,
`w2-final-reg-d3-recovered`). The pair did not charge $24 on Night 3; the bell did, and the desk's
own chart labels that same night `N3 $24 · 16,812 auto`. The cue is generated by
`dialCarriedLine` in `runtime/src/modules/fullHouse.ts:2513` — it matches on price value only
(`desk.nights.reverse().find(n => n.price === desk.price)`) and has no auto/manual predicate
(INFERRED from source). Same code path can also name a night older than last night. Not a crash and
not a lost night; it is a false attribution in the exact path a distracted or absent desk takes.

**2. ADVISORY — the dial does not carry, and the helper's own doc says it does.** OBSERVED on two
independent desks: `Desk 1` charged $34 on Night 1 and opened Night 2 at $24 (= plan) with no cue;
charged $48 on Night 2 and opened Night 3 at $24 again. The reset is deliberate
(`applyNight` returns `price: market.planPrice`, `fullHouse.ts:918`) and the view comment at 2508
states it, but the exported helper `dialCarriedLineFor` is documented "the dial keeps the last price
this desk charged" (`fullHouse.ts:1905`). Product behaviour is self-consistent; the stale comment
is a transfer hazard for the next builder, not a student-facing defect. Classroom risk that remains:
a pair that priced $48 opens the next night silently at $24 with nothing on screen saying the dial
moved back.

**3. ADVISORY — an unacknowledged result card is superseded, not queued.** With zero NEXT presses
across two consecutive bells, Desk 3's card was replaced each bell (N1 → N2 → N3). No night is lost
from the books or the chart (cash chain -$137,200 → $168,688 → $354,792; renewals 30% → 36% → 42%;
the Night 4 chart carries N1, N2 and N3), but a pair that never presses NEXT can only read a night's
card while it is on screen. Pacing choice, recorded not judged.

**4. ADVISORY — a device that rejoins by PIN resumes on the previous night's result card** even
when the seat's other device had already acknowledged it (OBSERVED `w2-final-reg-rejoin-2nd`:
`Desk 1`, Night 1 result card, books $215,384 / 30% identical to the seat's live state, while the
original device had already advanced to the Night 2 dials). Costs one extra NEXT press; coherent,
recoverable.

**No regression found** in: Module 1 rendering, refresh-mid-results, late join, double bell,
second-device rejoin, HOOK at either viewport, or a desk that never opens the bowl.
