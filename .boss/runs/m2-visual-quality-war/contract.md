## Hypothesis

The Module-2 design layer proven on /play in wave 2 can be propagated to the two shared classroom surfaces and the synthesis without weakening what wave 2 earned: /teach becomes a live class director to contract §F — fixed header with NIGHT N OF 5 and desk counts, filter chips, a desk-card grid that fits twelve desks at 1366x768 without wrapping collisions, a Director Rail (NOW / WATCH FOR / DON'T EXPLAIN YET / ASK / TIME CUT / RECOVERY) that scrolls inside itself with no facilitation line lost, and a persistent bottom control bar so nothing a teacher touches during a night sits below the fold (discharging baseline-browser-qa-dissent); /board gains a per-night class-results frame to §E2 with the legal columns only — DESK, TICKET PRICE, WHO CAME, FILL 'of the seats that desk opened' — no revenue or profit column, no per-desk money, drawn from the two Sports-Reality-verified markets only (disposing of bowl-rights-review-dissent), plus the §E1/§E4/§E5/§E6 frame grammar; and the synthesis carries R-5's computed visuals — realized class dots for one market and one card rather than a fitted curve, a two-axis frontier drawn from seasonFrontier and renewalMarginalCost rather than a balance scale, and DAY/DRAW/TV shifter chips with RENEWALS and LAST NIGHT'S EVENT MONEY in a separately labelled carried group and no 'Weather' — such that a fresh classroom-projector critic, a fresh teacher-transfer critic reading cold, and a fresh visual critic judge the three surfaces as one product, Economic Truth confirms every new rendered line is computed or registered and no projector claim outruns the model, /play and Module 1 are unchanged, and the full suite plus both M2 L1 e2e scripts stay green.

## Why this matters

Wave 2 proved the design system on the flagship student surface and left the classroom half of the product on the wave-1 baseline: a teacher whose pacing controls scroll out of reach, a projector whose largest figure is a lock counter, and a synthesis whose two most important pictures are still asserted rather than computed. A random competent teacher cannot run an outstanding class off a premium /play alone — the room runs on /teach and /board. This wave is also where the module's economics become publicly visible: the class-results frame is the first surface where one desk's decision is shown to the whole room, so the column law (no revenue, no profit, no per-desk money, fill always 'of the seats that desk opened') and the market-verification law are load-bearing, not cosmetic. Boss lead disposition on FD-1: the frontier visual is NOT held. Founder decision FD-1 asks whether Module 2's trade-off lesson is 'cheap fans first, ruinous fans last' rather than 'you cannot have both'; the honest drawing is the same picture under either answer, because the frontier is drawn from the model's own computed points and carries no caption asserting a trade-off size. If the founder answers FD-1 'no', what changes is the model's constants in a later wave with a fresh Economic Truth pass, not this drawing. Holding a required R-5 deliverable on an unanswerable question would stall the program against the founder's standing instruction to keep the loop running.

## Pass condition

A fresh Teacher Transfer critic, reading cold, judges that a random competent teacher who received this product tonight could run the class tomorrow, and finds every facilitation line that existed before this wave still reachable on the Director Rail; a fresh Classroom/Projector critic finds the three surfaces coupled, the reveal choreography intact with its manual fallback, and no seat-private data or per-desk money on the projector; a fresh Visual Experience Director grades /teach and /board at or above the grade /play holds at the wave-2 head and names any remaining gap as non-structural or as evidence for an already-recorded founder decision; Browser QA measures that no control a teacher touches during a night is below the fold at 1366x768 in any phase, discharging baseline-browser-qa-dissent with its owner's agreement; the class-results frame carries only DESK, TICKET PRICE, WHO CAME and FILL and names only Sports-Reality-verified markets, disposing of bowl-rights-review-dissent; Economic Truth confirms R-5's three computed visuals, R-6's teacher-surface exclusions and R-8, and finds no new rendered claim stronger than the model; the regression hunter finds /play and Module 1 unchanged; npm test and both M2 L1 e2e scripts are authentic passing command records at the final head, each claim citing evidence of the matching kind; and the analyst cannot disprove the above.

## Repair condition

An owning critic holds a material dissent that is bounded and repairable inside the wave's repair budget — a named contract row unmet, a control still below the fold, a facilitation line lost from the rail, a projector frame illegible or over-claiming, a computed visual still asserted — then repair and return the fix to the owning critic only, never to a critic who did not raise it.

## Rollback condition

Propagation breaks a sacred constraint that cannot be repaired without reopening the module's economics or the reveal choreography, or the projector leaks seat-private data in a way the frame's structure requires, or /play or Module 1's rendered output changes and cannot be isolated, or the e2e and misclick paths cannot be made green — restore checkpoint f320c8069e39784050f9eb9751e252962527fa94.

## Kill condition

Fresh critics find that the class-results frame or the Director Rail makes the live class harder to run than the wave-1 baseline on the same measured questions, with evidence that no adaptation inside the founder's allowed reasons fixes it; then the propagation thesis returns to the founder before any further build.
