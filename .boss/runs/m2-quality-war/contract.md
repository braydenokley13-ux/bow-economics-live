# Wave 1 contract

Run: `m2-quality-war`  
Level: 3 — QUALITY_WAR

## Hypothesis

Module 2 'Money in Motion' can become a coherent three-lesson sports-business module in which (1) the winning architecture is selected from genuinely competing candidates (A: evolve The Box Office; B: substantial refound preserving its verified insights; C: first-principles, sunk-cost-blind) via played browser prototypes, not prose comparison; (2) every flagship lesson rates STRONG or MAGNETIC on independent gameplay review; (3) every taught mechanism is economically true and instantiated in play before it is named; (4) real NBA/sports-business people, contracts, rules, and events are integral, dated, and never a fandom test; (5) a fresh-context teacher review finds each lesson runnable to an excellent standard without founder knowledge; and (6) the runtime survives classroom behavior on the three surfaces.

## Why this matters

Module 2 is the first module built under Economics Boss and the second module of Track 101. The existing Box Office prototype is technically verified but has never been gameplay/economics/teacher-gauntleted, and its architecture has never faced a competitor. Committing three lessons of build effort to an unexamined architecture is the exact sunk-cost failure the product philosophy forbids.

## Uncertainties

- Whether the Price Dial / hidden-demand-curve discovery loop is STRONG when actually played, or merely FUNCTIONAL.
- What the correct three-lesson economic arc for 'money in motion' is (revenue/price/demand -> ? -> ?), and whether revenue sharing and league institutions belong in it.
- Whether real historical sports-business situations (commit-then-reveal) beat operated-simulation lessons for one or more slots.
- Whether class interdependence (shared markets, competing prices) materially improves the economics or adds multiplayer cost without teaching value.
- Whether a random competent teacher can run a reveal-heavy economics lesson without founder knowledge.
- Whether Boss's workstream-lead machinery actually helps on a real product program (first live test).

## Required evidence

- Three genuinely independent candidate module architectures on file, each with per-lesson fantasy/verb/system/mechanism/real-sports/consequence/classroom/reveal/teacher/synthesis definitions.
- Played browser prototypes (browser-trace) for the uncertain signature loops of at least two competing candidates, with played-strategy notes (optimizing, random, cautious, aggressive, minimal-reading, replay).
- A dated sports-reality-report delivered before architecture selection, proposing real sports-business material as design input.
- An economic-truth-report covering mechanism truth, dominant strategies, false-lesson risk, and the full synthesis map for the winning architecture.
- gameplay-report per flagship lesson from an independent critic who played the browser build, rating STRONG or better.
- A fresh-context teacher-transfer-report (teacher-facing material first, builder context withheld) per lesson.
- projector-report treating the three surfaces as one session, including board privacy verification.
- visual-report against the premium sports-business media bar on rendered browser output.
- Passing test evidence recorded via evidence command (full runtime suite), plus rerunnable e2e proofs for each lesson arc.
- analyst-report attempting to disprove the program's claims against the recorded evidence.

## Non-goals

- Reopening or polishing Module 1 beyond narrow shared-runtime fixes a Module 2 regression forces.
- Module 3 or Module 4 work.
- World selectors, motif systems, commerce, enterprise, or Decision Challenges integration.
- A live sports-data ingestion architecture.
- Extracting a generic economics engine or generalizing the Boss harness.
- Redesigning or reinstalling the Boss harness itself.

## Sacred constraints

- Standing decisions D1-D19 hold; no silent mutation of the decision log.
- Four-module spine fixed (D1/D2); Module 2 stays 'Money in Motion' in the M2 slot.
- No XP/levels/badges/leaderboards (D4): the class reveal is the reward system.
- Scope is Track 101, grades 5-6 only (D6).
- Real-world sports business is mandatory product direction; sports knowledge may never be a prerequisite for the economics; reality never becomes a fandom test.
- Simplify the interface before simplifying the economics; material simplifications are recorded with misconception risk.
- boardView is structurally never handed a seat identity; /teach stays behind the per-session bearer key (D14).
- Every synchronized reveal ships a manual teacher fallback.
- Each module owns its state behind the LessonModule contract; no premature generic engine.
- A fun simulation that teaches false economics fails.
- Fictional student names only on shared screens.
- The founder controls merge to main; no auto-merge.

## Pass condition

All three lessons of the selected architecture are built, integrated, and independently verified: gameplay STRONG+ per flagship from played browser evidence; economic-truth report with zero unresolved blocking findings and a complete synthesis map; dated sports-reality report with no unresolved factual blockers; fresh-context teacher-transfer report finding no hidden-founder-knowledge blockers; projector/board privacy verified; full test suite and per-lesson e2e proofs green via evidence command; analyst reconciliation confirms the claims; ship case prepared for founder merge decision.

## Repair condition

The selected architecture holds under independent review but named findings (gameplay below STRONG on a flagship, a repairable economic-truth defect, a teacher-transfer gap, a runtime defect) remain within the repair budget and each has a bounded, evidence-backed repair path.

## Rollback condition

Integrated Module 2 work measurably degrades the shared runtime or Module 1's verified behavior (test or e2e regressions attributable to this program) and the degradation cannot be repaired within budget: revert to the recorded checkpoint.

## Kill condition

Independent play evidence shows the selected architecture's signature loop is WEAK/REFOUND at its core (not repairable by tuning), or the economics it teaches is false at the mechanism level, or no candidate architecture produces a flagship above FUNCTIONAL after the prototype war: kill or refound the module architecture rather than shipping it.
